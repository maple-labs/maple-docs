# Strategies

A Pool holds Lender deposits as idle cash until its Pool Manager puts them to work. In v7 a Pool allocates capital in one of two ways. It can fund open-term loans through the Open Term Loan Manager it owns, which only that Pool can use. Or it can fund a shared strategy through a Strategy Manager, which several Pools fund at once and which tracks each Pool's share of a single position in an Underlying Strategy such as Aave V3, Sky or an Allocation Strategy.

In both cases the Pool Manager treats the destination as a strategy: it transfers assets out through `requestFunds()`, enforces a per-strategy cap, and adds the strategy's reported value and unrealized losses into the Pool's `totalAssets()`.
