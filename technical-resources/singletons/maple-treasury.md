## Overview

The MapleTreasury contracts is how the Maple protocol collects and manages revenues.

Revenues collected by the MapleTreasury will be used to fund further development of the protocol.

## Access Control

The `governor` which is read from `MapleGlobals` has admin rights interact with the MapleTreasury including pulling funds using `reclaimERC20`.

## Revenue

Fees generated by the protocol are paid to the MapleTreasury. A more detailed breakdown can be found in the [fees](../protocol-overview/fees.md) section of the wiki.

This includes loan platform fees, strategy protocol fees, and what a strategy sends to the Treasury after a loss is recognized: sUSDS from the Sky Strategy's `recoverPosition()`, aTokens from the Aave V3 Strategy's `recoverPosition()`, and Aave rewards from `claimRewards()`.
